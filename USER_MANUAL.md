## Getting Started

### Creating an Account
1. Click the "Sign Up" button on the homepage
2. Fill in your email address and create a password
3. Alternatively, sign up using your Google account
4. Verify your email address (if required)
5. Complete your profile information

### Logging In
1. Click the "Login" button on the homepage
2. Enter your email and password
3. Or use "Sign in with Google"
4. You will be redirected to your dashboard

### Navigation
- **Navigation Bar**: Located at the top, provides access to all main features
- **Dashboard**: Your personalized workspace
- **Tools**: Quick access to all builders and tools
- **Services**: Access to design and 3D services
- **Blog**: Read articles and updates
- **Contact**: Get in touch with support

## CV Builder

### Creating a CV
1. Navigate to CV Builder from the dashboard or navigation
2. Choose a template from the available options
3. Fill in your personal information:
   - Contact details
   - Professional summary
   - Work experience
   - Education
   - Skills
   - Certifications
4. Use the live preview to see changes in real-time
5. Download your CV as PDF

### CV Features
- **Live Preview**: See your CV update as you type
- **Multiple Templates**: Choose from various professional designs
- **ATS Optimization**: Built-in ATS scoring to improve your chances
- **Export Options**: Download as PDF
- **Save & Edit**: Save your CV and come back to edit later

### Tips for a Great CV
- Use action verbs in your experience section
- Quantify achievements with numbers
- Keep it concise (1-2 pages)
- Tailor to the job you're applying for
- Use the ATS score to optimize your content

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

### Q: Can I use the platform for free?
A: Yes, we offer a free tier with limited features. Upgrade to access all features.

### Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings > Subscription > Cancel Subscription.

### Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data.

### Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes, you can export your data from Settings > Data Management.
